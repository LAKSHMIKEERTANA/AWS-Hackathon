HydroBalance
An interactive platform for understanding water use, heat, and energy sustainability.
Live demo: https://gldhasini.github.io/AWS-HACKATHON/
Overview
HydroBalance is an environmental sustainability project for the Heat and Water track. It explores the connection between energy generation and water resources, helping users understand how energy choices and cooling technologies can affect water use.
Problem
Water scarcity and rising temperatures put pressure on communities and industries. Some electricity-generation methods require water for cooling, while water treatment and supply also require energy. Understanding this water–energy relationship can support more sustainable planning.
What the project does
- Provides an interactive calculator for exploring energy-mix choices.
- Lets users compare cooling approaches and explore their potential effect on water use.
- Presents illustrative comparisons of water requirements across different energy sources.
- Encourages approaches such as renewable energy, efficient cooling, and suitable reuse of treated wastewater.
Note: Results shown by the calculator are educational estimates, not guaranteed real-world savings. Check the website's assumptions and data before using results for operational decisions.

Intended users
- Students and educators learning about environmental sustainability
- People interested in water conservation and clean energy
- Researchers and planners exploring the water–energy relationship
- Industry teams considering water-efficient energy practices

Run locally
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open index.html in a modern web browser.
If the project uses relative assets or browser features that require a local web server, start a simple static server from the project folder and open the local address it provides.
